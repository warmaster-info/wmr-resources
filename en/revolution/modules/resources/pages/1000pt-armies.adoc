= 1000pt Armies
:noindex:
:selector: https://selector.warmaster.info

Provided below are suggested 1000 point army lists for many of the _Warmaster Revolution_ armies.  Most were written by Zak Rohan, a prominent member of the UK Warmaster scene and a tournament organiser who knows the game very well, with additional contributions by users on the Warmaster Discord channel.

Each list presents a balanced army which can play multiple styles and can form the basis of a larger army.

See also xref:2000pt-armies.adoc[].
