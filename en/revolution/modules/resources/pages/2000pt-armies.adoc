= 2000pt Armies
:noindex:

Provided below are suggested 2000 point army lists for many of the _Warmaster Revolution_ armies.  They were written by Zak Rohan, a prominent member of the UK Warmaster scene and a tournament organiser who knows the game very well.

Each list presents a balanced army which can play multiple styles and can form the basis of a larger army.

See also xref:1000pt-armies.adoc[].
