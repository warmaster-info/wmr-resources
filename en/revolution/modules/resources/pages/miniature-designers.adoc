= Miniature designers
:page-role: resources
:files: 💾
:prints: 🧌
:casts: ⚓
:complete: 💯

Many independent artists and small businesses have designed miniatures suitable for using with _Warmaster Revolution_.

Some designers sell model files for personal 3D printing, these are marked {files}.  Most also licence their designs for printing by others, search the web for merchants selling printed models.

Other designers only sell miniatures, whether 3D printed (marked {prints}) or cast in resin or metal (marked {casts}).

Designers’ unit names may not correspond exactly to names in the army lists.  Entries marked with {complete} include miniatures covering all units in the army list (#TODO: Not yet done!#).
