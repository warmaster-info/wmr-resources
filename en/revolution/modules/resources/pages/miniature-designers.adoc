Many independent artists and small businesses have designed miniatures suitable for using with _Warmaster Revolution_.

Some designers sell model files for personal 3D printing, these are marked {files}.  Most also licence their designs for printing by others, search the web for merchants selling printed models.

Other designers only sell miniatures, whether 3D printed (marked {prints}), cast in metal or rarely resin (marked {casts}) or injection moulded (marked {plastic}).

Designs from different manufacturers can have slightly different proportions, styles, detail and overall quality.  As a rule, 3D printed resin miniatures will be more detailed than cast metal models, although the latter is more durable.

Designers’ unit names may not correspond exactly to names in the army lists.
